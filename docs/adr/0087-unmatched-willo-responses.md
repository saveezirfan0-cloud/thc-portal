# ADR-0087 · Unmatched Willo responses

**Status:** Accepted (incident, 05.10.2026). Builds on ADR-0021 (the receiver) and ADR-0066 (Willo's real API); an addition to scope v1.6 §2.4 and §2.12. **Deviation from the wireframe:** `wireframes/backoffice/onboarding.html` has no such panel.

## Context

A candidate finished the Willo interview on 4 Oct 2026 22:58 UK and appeared nowhere in the Back Office: no `staff`, `applications` or outbox row, and no `willo_invite_created` audit row, so the portal never created her in Willo. She reached Willo another way (the old Accelerate flow, an invitation sent by hand, a direct interview link). The `willo-webhook` function got `received` and `new_response` for her key, `willo_event_plan()` returned null, and the function answered 200 after writing one `audit_log` row, `willo_event_refused` / `unknown_willo_candidate`, with no entity and no name. Nothing in the office reads those rows. At least six other unknown keys had done the same since 2 Oct, some with `accepted` events from THC's own Willo reviewer. Real interview responses were vanishing without a trace.

Separately, `willo_record_failure()` (20260927161300) existed for the receiver's 500 paths but the Edge Function never called it.

## Decision

1. **The receiver keeps what the delivery said about a stranger.** `capturedApplicant()` (`packages/db/src/willo.ts`, vitest-held) walks the body defensively: the object holding the participant's key and two levels of parents first, then first/last/full-name and email fields anywhere, never under the reviewer, the interview or the company; a bare `name` only beside the key. `willo_record_refusal()` takes optional `p_name`, `p_email`, `p_occurred_at` (the old three-argument call still works; the function was replaced, not overloaded) and stores name and email **only when no staff row owns the key**. They are never logged; the delivery log stays values-free (`describeShape`). If the refusal row cannot be written the receiver now answers 500, so Willo retries, rather than 200 and a lost response.
2. **Every 500 is recorded.** The five 500 paths and the catch-all call `willo_record_failure()` (`plan_failed`, `record_failed`, `not_configured`, `provisioning_failed`, `accept_failed`, `refusal_not_recorded`, `unexpected`), best effort, then answer 500.
3. **One read for the office.** `willo_unmatched_responses(p_include_resolved)` (20261005110000): one row per Willo key with first and last seen, distinct events in order, name and email if captured, the review link from `settings.willo_review_url_template`, and whether it is resolved. A `security definer` function that refuses any caller who is not a Back Office login (`assert_office_caller`), not a view: the client role holds no policy on `audit_log` and sees nothing, `001_rls_guard` is untouched, and no table was added.
4. **State is audit rows, not a table.** A resolution is one more `audit_log` row, `willo_unmatched_linked` or `willo_unmatched_dismissed`. A response is resolved while its newest resolution is at least as new as its newest event, or while a non-removed candidate holds the key. A Willo event after a dismissal opens it again. A link is final.
5. **Link** (`willo_unmatched_link`, owners and managers, as ADR-0077): attaches the key to a candidate who is `interview_requested` or `interview_completed`, not removed and not already linked to Willo, then replays each distinct event once, oldest first, with Willo's own time, through `willo_record_event()`. New Response moves the card to Interview completed; Reject rejects and sends E2 (E2b if no interview was done); unmapped events are recorded as ignored. Linking also stops the invitation sweep creating that candidate in Willo a second time.
   **Accept is not replayed.** An Accept needs the candidate's login and a personal activation link that only GoTrue can mint, and E3 must never go out without one. The card is walked to Interview completed at the Accept's time and the result says `acceptPending`; the office's ordinary Accept (role pick, login, E3) finishes it, as after ADR-0077. Candidates past Interview completed are refused: replaying a stale Reject onto someone already taken on would reject them.
6. **Dismiss** (`willo_unmatched_dismiss`, owners and managers): reason mandatory, idempotent, refused for a linked response.
7. **Personal data.** On link or dismissal the stranger's name and email are removed from that key's refusal rows (they are on the staff row now, or not wanted). Both resolutions are audited with the actor and their name.
8. **The panel** on `/onboarding` ("Unmatched Willo responses", above the toolbar): only when there is an unresolved response, or when the read failed (coral alert — a failed read must not look like none) or the environment has no Supabase project (neutral note). Each row: who (or "Name not in the delivery"), the events as pills, first and last seen in UK time, **Open in Willo**, **Link to candidate**, **Dismiss**. The link dialog searches the board's candidates by name, email or phone, puts those matching the Willo details first, and spells out what linking will do for the events on file. Owners and managers see the actions (`canResolveUnmatchedWillo`); others see the list. A link or dismissal in flight dims the rows and disables the buttons.

## Consequences

- Migration `20261005110000_willo_unmatched_responses.sql`; pgTAP `774_willo_unmatched_responses`; vitest `willo-applicant.test.ts` and `apps/office/app/onboarding/__tests__/unmatched.test.tsx`.
- `audit_log` holds a stranger's name and email until the response is resolved. A response nobody resolves keeps them; the panel is how that is avoided.
- A person who applied only through the old flow and has no candidate here cannot be linked; dismiss with a reason (or create their application) is the route.
- Refusal rows written before this migration carry no name, email or Willo time; the panel shows them as "Name not in the delivery" and a link falls back to when the receiver saw each event. They need no backfill: the read works on the existing rows.
