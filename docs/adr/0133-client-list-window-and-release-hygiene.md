# ADR-0133 · The Client Portal list reads a recent window, and release hygiene for the portal

**Status:** Proposed · **Departs from:** `wireframes/client/events.html` (no "Show older events" line; a disabled "↓ Allocation Timesheet" is never drawn), `wireframes/client/event.html` (on-site contact "as given on your client card"; no contact button; the role pill reads "N of M confirmed"; no disabled header download) · **Source:** desk review 14.10.2026, and the live browser walk the same day (round 2, below)

## Context
The event list read every event, role section and line-up row the customer had ever had, with no order and no limit. PostgREST stops every response at `max_rows` (1000, `supabase/config.toml` and the hosted default) without an error, so after a few months for a busy venue faces vanished, the feedback nudge undercounted, and then sections and whole events dropped out — silently. Every refresh (once a minute, `AutoRefresh`) also shipped every worker's name the customer ever had, and re-signed every photo, so each face got a new URL and was downloaded again.

## Decision
- **The list reads a window** (`apps/client/app/client/window.ts`): every event that has not ended, in full; past events that started in the last 90 UK days (`PAST_WINDOW_DAYS`). A "From" filter replaces the start, a "To" alone pages the window back to end there, and `?since=` — written by **"Show older events"** under the list — widens it 90 days at a time. A date filter that moves the window re-reads on the server; the tab, search and venue still filter in the browser. Sections, line-up and documents are read only for the events loaded (`.in('event_id', …)`), every query explicitly ordered.
- **A cap is never silent.** Every read asks for one row more than it keeps (sections and line-up page through in 500s); the extra row coming back means "too many", which is the amber load-failed-style notice, never a short list. A test pins every "+1" read under `max_rows`.
- **An empty list with older history says so** ("You have no past events since 16 Jul 2026" + "Show older events"), never "No events yet".
- **Photo links are reused** for half their 10-minute life (a bounded LRU per server instance), and the list signs only the faces it draws. A signed URL carries the Storage path, so the worker's `staff.id` reaches the browser inside it; ADR-0026 §4 accepts that, and the comments no longer claim otherwise.
- **Event page:** "Contact THC about this event" (a mail link to the office address, subject = title · UK date · PO). The on-site contact reads "for this event · usually the contact on your client card" — it is per event (§3.2), pre-filled from the card and editable.
- **Hygiene:** `noindex, nofollow` (metadata, `robots.txt`, `X-Robots-Tag`); a branded `global-error.tsx`; the event title in the tab (the not-found title for an id that is not the caller's, so nothing leaks); a login with no company is told so; a lapsed session while sending feedback says "You've been signed out — sign in again" and keeps the comment. The office address is written once (`account/copy.ts` `OFFICE_EMAIL`).

## Consequences
- The Past tab's count is the count inside the window. Reaching older history is one click, or a "From" date.
- No view, policy or migration changed: the portal still reads only the owner-rights `client_*` views (ADR-0004, ADR-0026).

## Round 2 · the live browser walk (14.10.2026)

### Decision
- **The buffer never shows in a count (CW-01).** `client_role_sections_v.confirmed` counts every confirmed booking, buffer included, so a section of four carrying six read "6/4" and an event of Host 2/2 + Waiting Staff 6/4 + Bar Staff 0/2 read "8 of 8 confirmed", green, with the bar empty. Every count the customer reads now holds each SECTION to its headcount before adding (`rules.ts` `cappedConfirmed`): the list's "N of M confirmed" and its bar, the per-role split, the "Next up" strip, the event page's "Confirmed staff", the section pill, and "N of M arrived" (`arrivalsWithinHeadcount`; arrived is held to the capped confirmed). Green only when EVERY section is full. Two sections of one role are capped one by one, so a full lunch cannot hide an empty dinner. The line-up itself still lists everyone confirmed — they are who is coming.
- **The role pill reads "4 of 4 confirmed"** ("4 of 4 worked" once over), not the wireframe's "N confirmed": with six faces under a section of four, a bare "4 confirmed" read as a miscount.
- **No dead download (CW-04).** Until the office issues a copy there is no button, on the list or in the event header; a quiet line says what to expect — "Allocation Timesheet: sent the day before, once every role is confirmed" while the day before is still ahead (ADR-0088: the automatic send waits for a full line-up; the hour is an office setting, so it is not promised), otherwise "Allocation Timesheet not issued yet" / "Completed Timesheet not issued yet". "Details →" stays the row's way on.
- **Dates are typed dd/mm/yyyy (CW-03).** A native `<input type="date">` drew mm/dd/yyyy on a US-English device under "(UK date)". `UkDateField` is a text box with the browser's picker laid invisibly over a calendar icon (as ADR-0068's date of birth), writing only into the box; the address bar keeps `yyyy-mm-dd`. A bad entry is said in words once the box is left, and is not applied. A "From" after the "To" is its own empty state ("The dates are the wrong way round", with "Swap the dates") and an error on the "To" box.
- **Tab badges count live events (CW-14).** A cancelled event is still listed in its tab, greyed, and in the panel's "N events", but not in "Upcoming N" or "Past N".
- **After the event (CW-10)** the invitation reads "This event has finished — you can leave feedback for anyone you haven't yet", and "Add to calendar" is gone. Both go by the clock as well as the view's status.
- **Feedback a11y (CW-05–07).** Each button is named for its worker ("Leave feedback for Aisha Bello"). After a send the row turns "✓ Feedback sent" at once and focus lands on it; that control is `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`, so it can hold focus. Choosing a star clears "Choose a rating…"; the focused chosen star keeps its fill under a round ring, and the phone's tap flash is off.
- **The top bar is one row at every width above 760px (CW-02).** The brand never truncates; the company is the piece that gives way, with an ellipsis and its full name as the title. Below 1280px the zone note shortens to "Your time: UTC−4"; between 761 and 1000px the gaps tighten and the person's initials go (their account is under "Account"). A zone named by its offset reads "Viewer: UTC", not "UTC (UTC)".
- **Tablet (CW-08, CW-15).** With a coarse pointer up to 1024px every control in the bar and on both pages is at least 44px; a desktop mouse keeps the compact sizes. The table is kept from 768px, as the wireframe's tablet note says (cards ≤767px); it fits a 768px panel once the event's floor and the cell padding give a little — no deviation needed.
- **Downloads say they are working (CW-09).** A document link reads "Preparing…" (`aria-busy`) after a plain click until the page is shown again or 8 s pass; a modified click is the browser's own. The `?doc=missing|unavailable` note is shown once: the parameter is removed with `history.replaceState` and the note is held on the page until it is left.
- **Smaller:** the "Happening now" pill sits on the panel's ground (CW-11, 4.18:1 → about 5.1:1 in light, existing tokens); the panel title's qualifier takes its own line on a phone (CW-16).

### Consequences
- Still no view, policy or migration: everything above is presentation over the same `client_*` rows.
- `e2e/tests/client.portal.spec.ts` expects "↓ Download Allocation Timesheet" in the header "as a disabled button until then"; with no copy issued that now reads as the status line, so that assertion has to accept it. Its `getByRole('button', { name: 'Leave feedback' })` still matches the per-worker names. `client.phone.spec.ts`'s comment about cards below 820px is now ≤767px.
