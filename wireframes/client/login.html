Behaviour notes. Login (§1.4): back office and client both sign in with email + password on the web; a worker never does — they are activated through a personal link (public/activate.html) and live in the app. After sign-in the role decides the destination: Client → /client, Admin → /dashboard. A client URL typed by an admin (or the reverse) is refused, not redirected — the two apps never leak into each other. · Client users (§9.7, §11.4): the 2–3 contact people on the client card — the same addresses the allocation sheet and timesheet are emailed to. Access is created by the office; no self-registration. Everything behind the login is read-only and shows no money (§11.1). · Auth screens (§10.2): A0 Login · A1 Forgot password · A2 Reset link sent · A3 Set new password (shared landing, on public/activate.html). Reset emails come from admin@thehospitalitycompany.co.uk (§9.12); no no-reply address. · Responsive (§1.2): the Client Portal must work on tablet and phone — the card is the same component at 390px, full-width inputs, the "Forgot password?" link stays on the label row. · Assumptions to confirm: generic error wording; throttling after repeated failures; "Keep me signed in" duration.
