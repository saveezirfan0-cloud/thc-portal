Behaviour notes. Scope (§11.1, §1.4): client role only; the client sees ONLY their own events and only the confirmed line-up; everything is read-only — no editing anywhere. NO money: no pay rates, no charge rates, no margin, no totals, no invoice values. The client never sees the back office (§1.4). · Columns (§11.1): name · venue · date/time · status pill (Upcoming / Ongoing / Completed / Cancelled, §3.2) · "N of M confirmed" (N counts confirmed workers only, M is the booked headcount; buffer "+N" stays internal) · photos of the confirmed workers (real selfies, not initials — wireframe shows silhouettes) · a button to download the document · a link into the details. · Dual time zone (§1.8, ADR 0058): the scheduled window is shown as "07:00 – 23:30 UK time" plus a second "08:00 – 00:30 your time" line when the viewer's browser zone is not Europe/London; for a UK viewer the second line is not rendered at all (phone variant). Never a single unlabelled clock. The window is the event window — earliest role start → latest role end (RULE-18). · Document button (§11.3, §11.4): Allocation sheet (the blank form) is downloadable at any time before and during the event — no time restriction; once the event is Completed the button becomes Signed timesheet (the sign-out timesheet generated from check-in/out). Cancelled events keep their row, greyed, with no document. Download is a PDF so it can be dropped into WhatsApp; the "Send allocation sheet" email action lives in the Back Office, not here (§11.4). · GDPR (§1.7): a removed worker's row is not filtered out of a past event — it stays as "Deleted account #id" with no photo (see the dashed avatar on Waiting M&E), so the headcount is not skewed. · Status (§3.2): Ongoing = now between the event window's start and end; Completed once the window has ended (and the sign-out timesheet exists).
