Behaviour notes. Addition to Scope v1.6 (ADR-0083). 10/11 says "A copy of the signed agreement is kept on your profile"; this is that copy. my_contract() returns the version in staff.contract_version — the one signed — with its title and body exactly as published (versions are immutable, §2.11), and the signature stamp formatted in UK time (§1.8). Read-only. Reachable wherever Payment information is (canReachAgreement()): a working worker, a document-locked worker, and a leaver (§10.6 step 7). A candidate still in the wizard has no row. The screen shows the text and nothing else: it does not summarise the holiday clause, show a rate or calculate an accrual — the worker still sees the base rate only everywhere else (§9.8). A removed worker is refused (account_closed). The client sees nothing.
