Behaviour notes. Radar lists open shifts for the worker's own roles nearby right now, km badge, closest first (§10.4). Ordering mirrors auto-assign's two waves (RULE-17, §3.4): shifts at clients where the worker holds a ClientQualification for that role are grouped first under "You've worked here before" and are visible from the moment the role opens; shifts at other clients surface only once every eligible qualified worker has been invited and the role is still short — self-apply can't bypass the priority the invitations enforce. The detail shows the dress code before the worker decides; on-site contact and break policy only appear after booking (§10.4). Apply is blocked with "Limit Reached" when the shift would take the Mon–Sun week over the calculated cap (RULE-20, §4.4 — e.g. 18 h worked + 4 h > 20 h); the cap is derived live from the verified term letter and is never typed by anyone. Apply re-checks live availability: if the role filled since the screen loaded → "Sorry, this shift is now full", nothing recorded. An applied shift stays visible under "Applied" (purple) until it resolves: Confirmed → moves to Shifts + push N10; filled without them → leaves Radar + push N10c; event cancelled → N12 reaches pending applicants too (§8). A worker who self-cancelled off an event never sees that event on Radar again (RULE-04). Blocked / inactive workers don't see Radar at all (§4.3, §10.1). Self-apply is an extra channel — auto-assign keeps running (RULE-08).
