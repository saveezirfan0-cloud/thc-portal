Behaviour notes. Auth is four screens (§10.2): A0 Login · A1 Forgot password · A2 Reset link sent · A3 Set new password. No self-registration: a worker is activated through the personal link in E3 (accepted after the Willo interview, §2.7 "Activate · Activated", §8) and sets a password there; the public /activate page then hands off to the app. Reset and activation emails come from admin@thehospitalitycompany.co.uk (§9.12). A blocked, on-hold, quiz-rejected or leaver account can still sign in and lands on the corresponding lock screen (§10.1, see locks); a GDPR-removed worker cannot sign in (§1.7). PWA specifics: the app is installed to the home screen (iOS: Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: install prompt) and the Web Push permission must be granted, because the entire push register N1–N15 (§8) is delivered device-level and must reach the worker with the app closed (§10.5). Background GPS during a shift (§5.1) is the one native capability a PWA cannot fully replicate — flagged for the build, not a screen concern.
