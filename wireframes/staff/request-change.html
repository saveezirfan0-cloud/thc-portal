Behaviour notes. Additions to Scope v1.6 (ADR-0037 and ADR-0038, proposed — awaiting THC; docs/18 §2–§3). Request a change: name and photo stay locked (§10.1); the locked name row and the locked photo gain Request a change → /profile/details/request?kind=name|photo. A name needs first and last name (1–100 each), an evidence upload (signed upload into the documents bucket) and an optional note (≤ 500); a photo reuses the selfie camera. One pending request per kind; withdrawable while pending. The office approves or rejects on /staff/requests: approval updates the name (RC2 to the worker, RC4 to admin@ and payroll) or repoints the photo (RC2); a rejection needs a reason, which the worker sees (RC3). No automatic right-to-work re-check (Q13). Issued PDFs and payroll exports are never changed. Emergency contact: optional (Q11) — name, relationship (suggestions Parent / Partner / Sibling / Friend / Other), phone in E.164 with the international picker; Save and Remove. Only the office sees it (Staff profile, and later the check-in monitor); it is never on the allocation sheet, the timesheet or anything in the Client Portal. A leaver can read but not edit it; it is deleted on GDPR removal (Q12).
