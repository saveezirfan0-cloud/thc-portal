Invites — personal invitations: Accept / DeclineInvitations have no deadline and are never withdrawn by auto-assign; first to confirm takes the slot. Accepting auto-withdraws every other open invitation that overlaps its window (§3.4). Stale invites vanish on their own once the event has ended (RULE-16). Decline is always possible with no show-rate impact.

Behaviour notes. Invite card = role · event · date/time (own role window, RULE-18) · base rate/h · dress code · Accept / Decline, with confirm dialogs (§10.4). Dress code is shown before the worker decides; on-site contact and break policy are deliberately withheld until the shift is accepted (§10.4, §3.2, §5.2b). Invitations have no deadline and are never pulled by auto-assign (§3.6); first to confirm takes the slot — a late Accept gets "Sorry, this shift has been taken" and the invite moves to Closed (§3.4, shown on shifts). Accept → Confirmed and the system auto-withdraws every other open invitation that overlaps the window (they vanish from this list, shown as Booked elsewhere on the board). Safety net: Accept on a shift overlapping a just-confirmed booking is blocked with "You're already booked for an overlapping shift." — only confirmed bookings count, same venue back-to-back OK, different venues need a 2 h gap (§3.4). Weekly-hours hard gate: "Limit Reached" replaces Accept when the Mon–Sun week would exceed the calculated cap (RULE-20, §4.4). Decline is always allowed, no show-rate impact. RULE-16: an open invite disappears on its own once its event has ended. Pushes (§8): N5 on invite; N6 day-before with the 12:00 cutoff; N6b at the 12:05 automatic release; N11 on any start/end change → card gets "Time changed" + Awaiting + "Confirm new time" (§3.5); N12 event cancelled; N10b office Withdraw. Headcount, buffer, charge-rate or PO edits are silent.
