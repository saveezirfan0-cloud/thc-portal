Behaviour notes. Addition to Scope v1.6 (ADR-0039, proposed — awaiting THC; docs/18 §4). Offer this shift appears on a confirmed booking while more than 72 h remain (exactly the Cancel shift boundary, RULE-04), only while auto-assign is ON for the event and role, and only when no offer is open. The offerer stays confirmed: fill (confirmed only), buffer (shown as 6 (+1)), the line-up the client sees and every rule that counts bookings are unchanged. The offer closes at start − 72 h; if nobody took it the worker is still booked (OF3). Take this shift (/radar/offers/:id) runs in one transaction under the slot lock: the taker passes every hard gate auto-assign applies plus Accept's overlap and weekly-cap re-reads; RULE-17 holds (an unqualified taker is refused until wave 1 is exhausted — at once when auto-assign is off for the event or role, since no push ever goes out then). On success the taker is confirmed (source offer), the offerer's booking becomes cancelled with cause handed_over and the offerer is barred from the event as after a self-cancel (Q15); OF2 + OF4 go out; the taker's overlapping invitations are withdrawn as on Accept. Marking yourself unavailable (ADR-0036) never stops a take. Inside 72 h the worker can only Ask the office for cover (optional note); OF5 emails admin@ once per booking (asking again within 24 h of withdrawing a request is refused); the office opens it to the pool (until the section start), declines it (OF6) or withdraws the booking by hand. Any other exit from Confirmed (Withdraw, 12:05 cutoff, block, leave, GDPR, event cancelled, self-cancel) lapses the offer automatically. Radar's "Up for grabs" never names the offerer; the worker sees the base rate only. Every close time is a scheduled time: UK, labelled "(UK time)", with a "your time" line when the phone's zone differs (§1.8). Peer-to-peer offers and swaps are designed but off (Q17). The client sees nothing new.
