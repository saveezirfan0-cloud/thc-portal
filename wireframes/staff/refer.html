Behaviour notes. Addition to Scope v1.6 (ADR-0047, proposed — awaiting THC; docs/19 §5). The Profile row "Refer a friend" and this screen appear for compliant workers only. The link is {origin}/apply?ref={code}; the code is 8 characters from A–H, J–N, P–Z, 2–9, minted once and stable. Share uses the Web Share API; Copy link copies it; the QR is the existing activation QR component. The count is "N people have applied with your link" — never names or outcomes. No reward copy: THC has not decided one (Q19). A "your friend joined" push (RF1) is proposed, not built (Q20). On /apply the code travels in a hidden field on the service-role path only; the applicant never sees the referrer's name; the consent line and /privacy gain "If a friend referred you, we record who referred you." The office sees "Referred by {name} ({employeeId})" on /onboarding/:id, a "Referred" chip on the kanban card, and a Referrals card on /staff/:id. No money anywhere: nothing reaches PDFs, reports or payroll. The client sees nothing. On GDPR removal the code is revoked and the referral rows read "Deleted account #id".
