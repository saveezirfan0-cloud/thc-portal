Behaviour notes. Wizard = 11 steps with progress bar and N/11 counter; Continue is disabled until the step is complete (§10.3). 1/11 Right to work: the five branches and their document sets are exactly §2.5 pts 1–5 — nothing else is collected. DOB is mandatory in every branch; the share code is typed (never uploaded), validated as 9 alphanumerics starting with "W", case-insensitive, spaces stripped, then checked against gov.uk with the DOB (§2.5, §2.6) — the returned right-to-work-until date becomes the expiry used for reminders. The 48 h opt-out tick is offered to everyone but never overrides a visa limit (§4.4 table); in term time a student's 20 h/week stands regardless (RULE-20). 2/11 the pin gives the home ↔ venue distance used by scoring (§6) and Radar. 3/11 the selfie is the square avatar everywhere, printed on the timesheet, and locked after onboarding (§10.1). 4/11 documents upload → pending → verified / rejected-with-reason → Re-upload (§4.4); the AI pre-fills expiry and term dates but a manager confirms (§2.6). The criminal-conviction declaration sits on this step so there is one combined blocker: "No" is auto-verified on submit; "Yes" enters the manager's Needs-review queue with Verify/Reject like a document (§2.10, §4.1). File limits: PDF, JPG, PNG or HEIC, ≤10 MB. The quiz at 6/11 only unlocks once every document is verified (§2.9) — see the Documents hub on onboarding-3.
