Behaviour notes. Details (§10.4, §3.2): own role window only (RULE-18), venue address, dress code, on-site contact as free text exactly as entered on the event, notes/specific instructions, base rate. "Cancel shift" only while >72 h remain (RULE-04): dialog "We'll offer this shift to the next person on the list. This can't be undone." → slot released to auto-assign, no show-rate impact, worker permanently excluded from that event (§3.6). Check-in (§5.1): out of radius → red message with distance and disabled button; in radius → green and enabled; check-in during the 30-min grace = Late, paid from the scheduled start (RULE-01); 30 min after start → No-show, button locks, only a manager's "Get back" reclassifies (§9.5); a replacement confirmed after the start is exempt (§3.4). Strict buffer turn-away: on-time attempt logged → fixed 4 h pay, late → nothing (RULE-15). Breaks (§5.2b): shown only when the client does NOT pay breaks; Start break disabled before check-in ("Unlocks after check-in"); on break the break timer runs, the chargeable timer pauses, button reads "Finish break — back to work"; several breaks allowed, total deducted from pay and charge, listed in the timesheet Comments; the 6-hour banner is informational; N13 fires once at 6 h with no break. Client pays breaks → no buttons at all. Check-out: enabled from anywhere from the start until 4 h after the scheduled end; on site → actual time; off site → last on-site time from background tracking, or if none, the "No check-out" Violation and the "we couldn't confirm" copy (RULE-02); up to 15 min late still pays to the scheduled end (RULE-01); never pressed within 4 h → locks, Violation, static "we didn't receive your check-out" screen while the card stays in My shifts. Confirmation shows duration, rate and total earnings emphasised, base rate only, with the payroll copy (paid the Friday after the week worked, RULE-06). Static screens for cancelled (N12) / removed (N10b) / no check-out carry the contact line and "OK, I understand". Background GPS during the shift is the one native-only capability a PWA cannot fully honour — flagged for the build.
