Behaviour notes. Documents tab (§10.4): a row per document with Missing / In review / Verified (with expiry) / Re-upload (with the manager's reason, push N8), an Upload button and pull-to-refresh. Document lifecycle upload → pending → verified → expiring (≤30 d) → expired, or rejected → Re-upload (§4.4). The AI pre-fills the expiry (and term dates from the term letter) but a manager verifies (§2.6). The Official University Completion Letter is an optional row for the International student branch only; once verified it sets 48 h/week permanently and stops the term-letter reminder ladder (§4.5). Expiry pushes N1 (1 month) · N2 (2 weeks) · N3 (1 week) · N4 on the day, fully automatic (§4.2); the term letter always expires 31 Dec with reminders from 1 Dec. On expiry the system blocks the worker, releases all future bookings, withdraws invites, drops them from scoring and locks the app to the Documents tab (§4.3, §10.1 case 1); verifying any document re-runs the full compliance check and unblocks only when everything is verified and in date — nothing is restored. Declare a criminal conviction (§10.7): at the foot of the tab, set apart; required details, optional date, a plain notice, Submit → "Are you sure?"; on confirm a CriminalDeclaration (source = in-employment) is created, status → blocked, future bookings released, invites and Radar applications withdrawn, email E9 to the office without the details; the app locks to Documents with the "Thanks for telling us…" copy and the details are never shown back. Verify → full re-check → N15; Reject → converts to a manual block, the office calls. N14 announces every cap change (RULE-20, §4.4).
