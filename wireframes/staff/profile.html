Behaviour notes. Profile sheet (§10.1): opens from the avatar as a frosted-glass sheet; square selfie avatar, name, Employee ID, links Profile details / Security settings / Payment information, Sign out, the help line "Need help? Please contact us at: admin@thehospitalitycompany.co.uk" (not a separate screen), and "Request my P45" at the very bottom, below sign-out, separated and not styled as a primary action (§10.6). Profile details: name locked (tied to RTW and payroll — corrections via the office), avatar locked (set once at onboarding), NI masked ●●●●●●●2B and locked once entered (entering it for the first time sends E6); phone, email and home address editable — a new email is verified by a confirmation code before it replaces the old one; email and address changes send E7 to admin@ and thc_payroll@ (§8). Payment information: Earnings history = a card per completed, paid shift (role · rate/h · event · venue address · date/time · amount) with a "No earnings yet" empty state; base pay only — never holiday pay. Bank & payroll = the onboarding fields, "Save changes" → E5 to gisela@ and thc_payroll@ (§2.10). Request my P45 (§10.6): confirmation sheet "Leaving The Hospitality Company?" states the consequences in full, optional reason, Cancel + non-default "Yes, request my P45", then "Are you sure? This can't be undone from the app". Disabled while checked in ("Available once you've checked out"). On confirm: status → inactive with left_at and reason; every future booking released to auto-assign; open invites and Radar applications withdrawn; out of the scoring pool; E8 to the office immediately; the app locks to the leaver screen with only Payment information reachable. Nothing is anonymised (GDPR Remove is separate, §1.7); coming back = Reset to candidate, same record, same Employee ID, full wizard again (§2.12). Employee ID format is not specified — THC-00417 is a placeholder.
