Behaviour notes. App lock cases (§10.1): (1) Auto-block on documents — not compliant or an expired document (§4.3), a declaration under review (§10.7), or anything unverified after onboarding (§10.3): the worker signs in but sees ONLY the Documents tab; Shifts, Invites and Radar are locked until every document is verified and in date and any "Yes" declaration is verified — the full compliance re-check runs on every Verify and unblocks automatically, with no restoration of released shifts. (2) Manual block by a manager (§9.6): nothing for the worker to fix, so no Documents action — a static "Your account is on hold. Please contact the office at: admin@thehospitalitycompany.co.uk" screen; the reason is internal and never shown; only Unblock (which also runs the full compliance check) lifts it. A rejected conviction declaration converts to this manual block (§10.7). (3) Rejected after the third quiz failure (§2.9): the wizard is replaced by the terminal screen with THC's copy, identical to email E4. (4) Leaver via Request my P45 (§10.6): the leaver screen, Shifts / Radar / Invites / Documents closed, Payment information (earnings history) still reachable. GDPR-removed: cannot sign in (§1.7). Blocked, inactive and removed workers receive no invitations and leave the scoring pool (§2.12). Push register (§8): the main channel is push — email survives only before the app exists (E1–E3) or for Willo; all N1–N15 are device-level and, in the PWA, delivered as Web Push, which is why the permission screen on auth is mandatory.
