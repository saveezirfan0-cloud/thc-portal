Behaviour notes. Addition to Scope v1.6 (ADR-0043, proposed — awaiting THC; docs/19 §1). An entry is one day (all day = UK midnight to UK midnight, so 23 h or 25 h on a clock-change day), a date range of up to 31 days, or a time window on one day in UK time — labelled "(UK time)", with a "your time" line when the phone's zone differs (§1.8); a window whose end is at or before its start runs overnight. "Repeat weekly" makes up to 26 entries sharing one series, keeping the UK wall-clock time across a clock change. Limits: nothing in the past, nothing more than 12 months ahead, at most 200 future entries. No reason field (Q10). What it does: auto-assign's first round, hourly rounds, cutoff refills, same-day escalation and offer pushes (OF1) skip a worker whose entry overlaps the role section window (RULE-18). What it does not do: withdraw an open invitation (§3.4), cancel a confirmed booking (the worker is warned and pointed at Cancel / Offer), or stop the worker accepting, applying on Radar or taking an offered shift. A manager can still invite by hand after "{name} marked themselves unavailable for this time. Invite anyway?" (Q9). The office sees the entries read-only on /staff/:id → Availability. The client sees nothing. GDPR removal deletes the entries.
