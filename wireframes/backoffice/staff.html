The cap is never typed or stored — it is derived on the date it is evaluated from the verified term dates (RULE-20, §4.4): 20 h in term, 48 h in a holiday range, 48 h permanently once a completion letter is verified (§4.5); the 48h opt-out cannot lift the in-term visa limit. Push N14 tells the worker on the morning a band changes (§7 compliance_daily).
Behaviour notes. Directory columns: photo · name (clickable → profile) · role(s) · rating (0–2.9 red, 3.0–3.9 amber, 4.0+ green) · reliability / show-rate · compliance status; a separate "Limit reached" badge next to the status when the calculated weekly cap for the current Mon–Sun week is reached, with cap and reason on hover — a temporary per-week condition, not a Staff.status value (§9.6, RULE-20). Filters All · Compliant · Blocked · Inactive · Removed, search and pagination (§9.6). Inactive lists everyone who left via the app, newest first, with the date they left and the reason they gave, so the office can work through P45s and final pay (§9.6, §10.6). Removed rows are not filtered out — they read "Deleted account #id" with name, photo and contacts anonymised while roles and rating remain (§1.7). The dedicated Student visa view lists every worker on the International student branch with their current weekly cap, the evidence set that produced it (term letter or completion letter, with verification date) and their right-to-work expiry (§4.5).
