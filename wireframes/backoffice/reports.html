Behaviour notes. Three tabs (§9.9). Financial: defaults to the current Mon–Sun week with a "This week" button + arbitrary range; KPIs Staff payroll (base and the 12.07% holiday element broken out and labelled separately, never blended) · Client invoicing at the charge rate · Gross margin; payable hours = intersection of check-in/out with the scheduled time (RULE-01); invoicing is a revenue forecast, not the PO invoices; the "Forecast for the period" label shows for the current week and the previous week through Tuesday. Payroll: date range + "Last week"; period summary (workers · shifts · total · hours); per-person rows Staff · Employee ID · Shifts · Payable hours · Base · Holiday · Total payroll; expanding a person lists ALL their shifts with Event · Client · Role · Date · Start–End (scheduled) · Check in/out (actual, late check-in / early check-out in amber) · Payable hours (unpaid break deduction shown) · Rate · Payroll; buffer turn-aways read "Turned away" (fixed 4 h, RULE-15); an unresolved No check-out reads "Pending" and is excluded from the CSV (BG-08). CSV = one row per shift, never averaged, Employee ID on every row. New Starter (HMRC): pick a date → preview → Export CSV; columns Staff · Employee ID · NI Number · Home address · Postcode · Country · Date of birth · Gender · First shift date · HMRC Statement (A/B/C) · Student Loan; only new workers who actually worked last week. Automatic send-out every Monday 09:00 — one email, payroll CSV always, HMRC CSV only if there are new starters; each tab shows "Last sent: [date], [time]", "Failed to send report" or, for HMRC, "No new: [date], [time]" (§9.9). Week boundaries follow the wireframe anchor Thu 18 Sep 2026 (Mon 15 – Sun 21).
