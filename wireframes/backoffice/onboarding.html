Behaviour notes. Six columns, confirmed against the approved "Onboarding / Kanban / Active" design (§2.7): Interview requested → Interview completed → Documents → Quiz → Additional info → Contract. Cards carry the selfie avatar (initials here), name, days in the current stage, and from Documents onwards the qualified role chips the manager picked right after the Willo acceptance (§2.4, §9.6). "Review interview on Willo" is a direct link on the two interview columns — the decision is made in Willo and reaches the system through the webhook (New Response → completed; Stage Change → Accept / Reject, §7). Documents → Quiz is automatic once every document and a Yes Criminal Record declaration are verified (§2.3, RULE-10). Quiz: 80% pass mark, 3 attempts, third failure rejects automatically (RULE-09). Contract signed → Employee ID generated, card leaves the board (§2.7). Active / Rejected toggle per §2.2. The amber "Returning applicant" card is the duplicate-check route of §2.12: it names the existing record, the manager either presses Reset to candidate (same record, same Employee ID, full wizard again, compliance evidence superseded) or rejects; a GDPR-removed worker cannot be matched and applies as new.
