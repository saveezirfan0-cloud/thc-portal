Behaviour notes. Additions to Scope v1.6 (docs/19; ADR-0042, ADR-0043, ADR-0044, ADR-0046 — proposed, awaiting THC). /staff/requests (ADR-0044): pending requests oldest first, current and requested side by side (photos by signed URL), evidence link and note. Approve a name only after ticking "I've checked the evidence matches the right-to-work document" — writes the name and the audit log, pushes RC2, emails RC4 to admin@ and payroll; no automatic right-to-work re-check (Q13). Approve a photo repoints the profile photo despite the lock; the old file is kept. Reject needs a reason, shown to the worker (RC3). A decided request cannot be decided again. Issued PDFs and payroll exports are never changed. /staff/:id: a pending-request banner; an Emergency contact card (Edit / Clear, audited, "Not provided" when empty — ADR-0043); a Referrals card and "Referred by" (ADR-0046); a read-only Availability tab for the next 8 weeks in UK time (ADR-0042). The Client Portal, the allocation sheet and the timesheet show none of this. Stamps are UK time (§1.8).
