Behaviour notes. Client feedback and office feedback are two separate tabs, never a combined list (§9.10). Search by staff name on both. Client feedback arrives from the Client Portal (§11.2): stars + a comment tied to a worker and an event; a list with Mark as read — this action exists only here; the channel is read-only (no reply, edit or delete), and the entry counts toward the rating only once marked read. Office feedback: the manager gives a worker stars + a comment (worker search, stars, comment, optional event); entries can be edited or deleted after submission, here and on the worker's profile; the author shown is the manager's own name; it counts toward the rating immediately. Both types feed the rating that is 25% of the auto-assign score (§6). On GDPR removal, comments are retained verbatim and the office redacts a name by editing an office entry or deleting a client entry if asked (§1.7).
