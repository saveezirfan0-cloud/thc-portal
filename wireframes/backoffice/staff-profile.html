Two kinds of block

§9.6
Auto-block (expired document, or an in-employment conviction declaration) — system-triggered, temporary, no reason needed; lifts by itself after verification + the full re-check. Label reads e.g. "Blocked — passport expired 02.09.2026".
Manual block (this one) — a manager pressed Block and gave a reason; it always means something went wrong; only a manager's Unblock lifts it. The worker never sees the reason.
A worker who has simply left is not blocked — they land in Inactive via the app (§10.6).
