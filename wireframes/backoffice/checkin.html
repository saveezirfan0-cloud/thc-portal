Actual finish (UK time) *
Finish time cannot be before the worker's check-in (17:04) Entered in UK time — every other time in this window is in your zone, so the label says which is expected (§1.8). Server rejects a value before the check-in or in the future and keeps the dialog open with the reason. No upper bound against the scheduled end: RULE-01 caps pay at 01:00 UK regardless.
Note *
On Resolve: the entered time becomes the shift's check-out for RULE-01 → payable time fixed → the monitor row reads "Checked out [time]" → the 4 h floor applies again and the violation stops counting against show-rate → the shift goes into the next Monday 09:00 payroll CSV (§5.2, §7 BG-08). Regenerating the timesheet fills in his Finish Time and Hours Worked (§11.3).

Behaviour notes. Top half: today's live state — Staff (photo + name) · Event · Window (the worker's own role window, dual-zone: "UK time" line + "your time" line, the second omitted for a UK viewer, §1.8 / ADR 0058) · Check-in (actual stamp, viewer local, single line) · Breaks ("N · last HH:MM"; a dash where the client pays breaks, never "0" for no data) · Status (§9.5). Status pills: On shift (green) · Off-site (amber — background GPS detected a geofence exit mid-shift, live on the table) · Checked out [time] (neutral; red when the recorded departure is more than 15 min after the scheduled end, until 4 h) · No check-out (red, raised at +4 h, RULE-02) · Due [start] (amber, local time only, no zone suffix — deliberate) · Not confirmed today (amber, skipped the on-the-day confirmation, can still check in) · Not checked in — 30 min alert (red, same label through the auto No-show at start + 30, BG-03). The −1 worker flag is carried on the event (§9.5, §7). Red lives on the pill, never as a row background. Bottom half: Violation log Staff · Event · Violation · Time, coral highlight, newest first; row clickable + "Details" as the keyboard path; "Show resolved" checkbox unchecked by default. Types: No-show · Late · Left early · Left the geofence during the shift · No check-out; the first two of the automatic ones (No-show at +30 min, No check-out at +4 h) are system-raised, the other three reviewed case by case. Every type has Resolve with a mandatory note; No-show Resolve = "Get back"; No check-out Resolve additionally needs "Actual finish (UK time)", server-validated (not before check-in, not in the future), which then becomes the check-out for RULE-01. Route maps are never shown. The detail window doubles as the audit trail — note, manager name and (for No check-out) the entered finish time stay visible (§9.5). The same log, scoped to one worker, lives on the staff profile's Shifts tab (§9.6).
