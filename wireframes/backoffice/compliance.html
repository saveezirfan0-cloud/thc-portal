What the block does · automatically

§4.3
1. Status → blocked · 2. removed from ALL future confirmed shifts → slots released to auto-assign · 3. open invitations withdrawn · 4. out of scoring · 5. app locked to the Documents tab.
Unblock: the worker re-uploads → the office verifies in Needs review → the FULL compliance status is re-checked (every document in date + a Yes declaration verified) → unblocked automatically. No manual Unblock for an auto-block; a manual block needs the manager's Unblock on the profile (§9.6).
Behaviour notes. Two tabs (§4.1). Needs review lists every profile — candidate or staff — with a document, or a Criminal Record declaration answered Yes (onboarding or in-employment, §10.7), in the under-review state; Rejected candidates and Removed workers drop out automatically. Each row: who · which document · when it was uploaded · what the AI found (extracted date + confidence, or "needs manual review") · Verify / Reject. Reject asks for a reason → push N8 with a Re-upload button. Verify confirms the document; for a blocked worker it triggers the full compliance re-check of §4.3 — this one document alone does not unblock them if anything else is outstanding. The menu carries a counter (7). The Official University Completion Letter goes through this same queue (§4.5): verifying it sets graduated_at, turns the cap to 48 h/week permanently, stops the term-letter reminder ladder and sends N14. Radar: counters expired (blocking) / expiring; table who · document · expiry date · days left · status. Blocking is fully automatic and there is no manual "Send reminder" — the push ladder (1 month, 2 weeks, 1 week, expiry day) runs from the daily job (§4.2, §7 BG-04/05). University Term Dates Letters expire 31 December regardless of printed dates, with reminders from 1 December; an already-expired letter is not accepted. Right-to-work-until from gov.uk is the expiry used for share-code reminders (§4.4).
