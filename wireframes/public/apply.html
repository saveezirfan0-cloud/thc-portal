What happens next for the candidate
Apply → Willo email (E1) → video interview → decision inside Willo → accepted: activation email (E3) → set password → install the app → 11-step onboarding (Appendix A).
Behaviour notes. Fields (§2.1): First name · Surname · Email · Mobile (international country-code picker) · Age (select from 18) · GDPR consent. Public URL, no registration, no login. · Age gate (§2.1, §1.7, Appendix A step 1): under 18 is rejected on the spot — coral error on the form, submit disabled — and the server repeats the check, so a tampered form still fails. The select lists 18 upward; a single "Under 18" option exists only so the form can reject honestly instead of hiding the case. · No "Applied" stage (§2.1, §2.7): a valid submission creates the candidate directly in Interview requested; the system creates them in Willo and Willo sends the interview invitation (E1) itself. The kanban card then moves on its own when the interview is completed (§2.4). · Duplicate check (§2.12): on submission the system matches email, and mobile + date of birth, against existing records. A match does not create a second candidate — it is routed to the office as a "returning applicant" entry on the Onboarding screen naming the existing record; the manager presses Reset to candidate or rejects. The applicant sees the ordinary "Check your inbox" screen either way and is never told why a previous record was blocked. A GDPR-removed worker (§1.7) cannot be matched and applies as a genuinely new candidate. · Open point: §2.1 collects Age while §2.12 matches on mobile + DOB — the form has no DOB field, so either DOB is added to /apply or the mobile+DOB match relies on the DOB entered later in the app (§2.5). Flagged for THC. · Consent (§1.7): the GDPR tick-box is mandatory; its timestamp is stored with the record. · Emails: the interview invitation comes from Willo; everything else from admin@thehospitalitycompany.co.uk (§9.12).
